Kantonstour

One beer in every canton in three days, entirely by rail — start and end Zürich HB, first night Luzern, second night Biel/Bienne. You hop off in all 26 — two ways to pace it: Option A with relaxed stops of at least 30′, or Option B tight: 15′ is enough for a station beer, and a stop only gets longer where the hourly timetable forces it. Pure train plan — every connection is from the official SBB timetable for these exact dates (tap SBB on any leg to open it live); food and hotels are up to you.

Verdict: possible — 26/26 cantons, 26 station stops, home 16:58 (A) or 16:28 (B)

The two walk links

part of the route
Altstätten Stadt → Altstätten SG (Friday, 42′ window). The Appenzeller Bahnen rack railway ends at Altstätten Stadt; the SBB Rheintal station is ~1.7 km further down — an easy downhill walk of ~20–25 min. This is what avoids passing through St. Gallen twice.
Ziegelbrücke (Friday, 35′ window in A, 29′ in B). The station itself already stands on Glarus soil, but for an honest GL beer walk ~5 min over the Linth channel into Niederurnen (Glarus Nord) and back.

Good to know

verified 23.08.2026
Everything runs hourly — the schedule is a chain. Miss one train and you usually lose 60 minutes. Set phone timers. Option B's 15–20′ stops leave no margin for a late train — if one slips, fall back to the next hourly service and the rest of the chain shifts by an hour.
Day lengths: Option A — Fri 07:05–23:16, Sat 07:18–22:41, Sun 08:18–16:58. Option B — Fri 07:05–20:46, Sat 07:18–21:41, Sun 08:18–16:28. Both sleep Luzern, then Biel.
The RE 48 briefly crosses Germany (Jestetten corridor before Schaffhausen). Swiss tickets valid, normally no checks — carry an ID.
The CJ leg is a train, not a bus: the narrow-gauge red Chemins de fer du Jura from Glovelier through the Franches-Montagnes to La Chaux-de-Fonds. Option A rides it 19:41→20:56, Option B 18:41→19:56 (dusk) — 97′ through the Jura, the best ride of the trip.
Drinking your own beer on board is allowed on all trains in this plan (SBB, Thurbo, SOB, Appenzeller Bahnen, Zentralbahn, CJ) and on platforms.
Tickets: three Saver Day Passes (buy early) or a GA — one pass covers everything here, including Appenzeller Bahnen, Zentralbahn and CJ.
OLMA fair runs in St. Gallen 8–18 Oct — expect very full trains around St. Gallen on Friday. Re-check the timetable a few days before (sbb.ch) — the SBB buttons on each leg open the live connection.
